The [recent disappointing news](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare)
that Deno's team would be moving to Cloudflare and ceasing all further work
on the Deno runtime meant I really needed to move Raptor's toolchain.

The framework already supported Node out of the box, just like it supported
Deno and Bun. But the toolchain itself was running Deno, and that needed 
ported.

While I was moving the toolchain, I had a bit of time to look into micro-optimisations.
I added a new lightmode to the Kernel, which swaps the Web Standard `Request` object in
favour of a lighter stand-in, resulting in 30% better performance in benchmark tests.

As an aside, despite claims that they would continue to support JSR, I decided it was better to just
move the project over to NPM, just in case. It's now sitting at `@raptor-js/kernel`.
